# Planetary Meanings in Astrology

## Sun
The Sun represents your core identity, ego, and life force. It shows who you are at your deepest level.
In a birth chart, the Sun's sign and house reveal your conscious self-expression and creative energy.
The Sun rules Leo and is exalted in Aries. It takes about one year to transit the zodiac.

## Moon
The Moon governs emotions, instincts, and your inner world. It reflects your emotional needs and how you nurture yourself and others.
The Moon's placement reveals your subconscious patterns, habits, and what makes you feel secure.
The Moon rules Cancer and is exalted in Taurus. It transits a sign every 2.5 days.

## Mercury
Mercury rules communication, intellect, and mental processes. It shows how you think, learn, and express ideas.
Mercury's sign and house reveal your communication style, analytical abilities, and learning preferences.
Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo. It stations retrograde about 3-4 times per year.

## Venus
Venus governs love, beauty, pleasure, and values. It shows what you attract and how you express affection.
Venus reveals your relationship style, aesthetic tastes, and what brings you joy.
Venus rules Taurus and Libra. It goes retrograde approximately every 18 months.

## Mars
Mars represents action, drive, ambition, and assertion. It shows how you pursue goals and express anger.
Mars reveals your energy levels, competitive nature, and physical drive.
Mars rules Aries and is co-ruler of Scorpio. It goes retrograde about every 2 years.

## Jupiter
Jupiter is the planet of expansion, luck, wisdom, and growth. It shows where you experience abundance and opportunity.
Jupiter's placement reveals your philosophical outlook and capacity for optimism.
Jupiter rules Sagittarius and Pisces. It spends about one year in each sign.

## Saturn
Saturn represents discipline, responsibility, limitations, and life lessons. It shows where you face challenges that lead to maturity.
Saturn's placement reveals your sense of duty, fears, and areas requiring patience and hard work.
Saturn rules Capricorn and Aquarius. It spends about 2.5 years in each sign.

## Uranus
Uranus governs innovation, rebellion, sudden change, and individuality. It represents awakening and breakthroughs.
Uranus reveals where you challenge norms and experience unexpected shifts.
Uranus rules Aquarius. It spends about 7 years in each sign.

## Neptune
Neptune rules dreams, intuition, illusion, and spirituality. It dissolves boundaries and connects to the divine.
Neptune's placement reveals your imagination, spiritual leanings, and where you may experience confusion or transcendence.
Neptune rules Pisces. It spends about 14 years in each sign.

## Pluto
Pluto governs transformation, power, death and rebirth, and deep psychological processes.
Pluto reveals where you undergo profound change and confront shadow aspects of yourself.
Pluto rules Scorpio. It spends 12-31 years in each sign.
