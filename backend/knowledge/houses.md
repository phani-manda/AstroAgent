# Astrological Houses

## 1st House - House of Self
The Ascendant or Rising sign. Governs self-image, physical appearance, and how others perceive you.
Planets here strongly influence your personality and approach to life.

## 2nd House - House of Value
Governs material possessions, finances, self-worth, and personal resources.
Reveals your relationship with money and what you truly value.

## 3rd House - House of Communication
Governs communication, siblings, short journeys, and early education.
Reveals your thinking style and how you process information.

## 4th House - House of Home
The IC (Imum Coeli). Governs home, family, roots, and emotional foundations.
Reveals your private self and ancestral patterns.

## 5th House - House of Creativity
Governs creativity, romance, children, pleasure, and self-expression.
Reveals how you play, create, and experience joy.

## 6th House - House of Health
Governs daily routines, work, health, and service to others.
Reveals your approach to wellness and practical duties.

## 7th House - House of Partnerships
The Descendant. Governs committed relationships, marriage, and open enemies.
Reveals what you seek in a partner and your approach to one-on-one connections.

## 8th House - House of Transformation
Governs shared resources, intimacy, death and rebirth, and the occult.
Reveals deep psychological processes and transformative experiences.

## 9th House - House of Philosophy
Governs higher education, travel, religion, philosophy, and belief systems.
Reveals your quest for meaning and expansion of consciousness.

## 10th House - House of Career
The MC (Midheaven). Governs career, public image, reputation, and life direction.
Reveals your professional path and what you aspire to become.

## 11th House - House of Community
Governs friendships, groups, hopes, wishes, and social causes.
Reveals your relationship with community and collective ideals.

## 12th House - House of the Unconscious
Governs the subconscious, spirituality, hidden matters, and self-undoing.
Reveals your inner world, dreams, and connection to the divine.
