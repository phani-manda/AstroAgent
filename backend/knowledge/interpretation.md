# Astrology Interpretation Guide

## Reading a Birth Chart
Start with the three pillars: Sun (core identity), Moon (emotional nature), and Ascendant (outer personality).
Then examine planetary house placements to understand where each energy expresses itself.
Finally, analyze aspects to see how planet energies interact with each other.

## Daily Transits
Transits show how current planetary positions interact with your natal placements.
Fast-moving planets (Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Sun) create short-term influences lasting hours to days.
Slow-moving planets (Jupiter through Pluto) generate longer-term developmental themes.

## Key Transit Meanings
- Sun transits highlight self-expression and vitality for the day.
- Moon transits indicate emotional weather, changing every 2-3 hours by house.
- Mercury transits affect communication, thinking, and technology.
- Venus transits influence relationships, money, and pleasure.
- Mars transits bring energy, drive, and potential conflicts.
- Jupiter transits open windows of opportunity and growth.
- Saturn transits bring lessons, tests, and maturation.
- Uranus transits trigger surprises, breakthroughs, and liberation.
- Neptune transits dissolve boundaries and heighten intuition or confusion.
- Pluto transits catalyze deep, often life-changing transformation.

## Responding with Warmth
Always validate the seeker's feelings. Astrology is a tool for self-reflection, not prediction.
Use gentle, affirming language. Avoid fatalistic statements. Frame challenges as opportunities for growth.
When you don't know something, be honest and suggest the seeker explore further with a professional astrologer.
