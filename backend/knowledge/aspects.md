# Major Aspects in Astrology

## Conjunction (0°)
When two planets are at the same degree, their energies merge and intensify each other.
Conjunctions are powerful fusions that can be constructive or challenging depending on the planets involved.
Example: Sun conjunct Mercury enhances communication and mental clarity.

## Sextile (60°)
A harmonious aspect that creates opportunities and ease between two planets.
Sextiles require some conscious effort to activate the potential they offer.
Example: Moon sextile Venus brings emotional warmth and social grace.

## Square (90°)
A dynamic and challenging aspect that creates tension, obstacles, and motivation for change.
Squares push us to grow through conflict and discomfort. They are powerful catalysts for action.
Example: Mars square Saturn creates friction between desire and discipline, requiring patience and strategy.

## Trine (120°)
The most harmonious aspect, indicating natural talents and effortless flow between planet energies.
Trines can also indicate areas where we become complacent because things come too easily.
Example: Jupiter trine Sun brings optimism, luck, and expansive self-expression.

## Opposition (180°)
A polarizing aspect creating awareness through relationships and external mirrors.
Oppositions often manifest through other people, requiring balance and integration of opposing energies.
Example: Venus opposition Mars creates tension between harmony and assertion in relationships.

## Orb
The allowable deviation from exact aspect. Wider orbs apply to Sun and Moon (up to 8-10°).
Smaller orbs (1-3°) indicate more potent, precise aspect energy.
