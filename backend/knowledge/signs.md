# Zodiac Signs and Their Meanings

## Aries (March 21 - April 19)
Cardinal Fire sign ruled by Mars. Pioneering, courageous, direct, and enthusiastic.
Keywords: initiative, leadership, independence, impulsiveness.
House association: 1st House.

## Taurus (April 20 - May 20)
Fixed Earth sign ruled by Venus. Stable, sensual, patient, and persistent.
Keywords: security, pleasure, determination, materialism.
House association: 2nd House.

## Gemini (May 21 - June 20)
Mutable Air sign ruled by Mercury. Curious, adaptable, communicative, and witty.
Keywords: versatility, intellect, duality, restlessness.
House association: 3rd House.

## Cancer (June 21 - July 22)
Cardinal Water sign ruled by the Moon. Nurturing, protective, emotional, and intuitive.
Keywords: home, family, sensitivity, memory.
House association: 4th House.

## Leo (July 23 - August 22)
Fixed Fire sign ruled by the Sun. Confident, generous, theatrical, and warm-hearted.
Keywords: creativity, leadership, pride, loyalty.
House association: 5th House.

## Virgo (August 23 - September 22)
Mutable Earth sign ruled by Mercury. Analytical, practical, modest, and service-oriented.
Keywords: precision, health, duty, perfectionism.
House association: 6th House.

## Libra (September 23 - October 22)
Cardinal Air sign ruled by Venus. Diplomatic, graceful, fair-minded, and sociable.
Keywords: balance, partnership, harmony, indecision.
House association: 7th House.

## Scorpio (October 23 - November 21)
Fixed Water sign ruled by Pluto and Mars. Intense, passionate, secretive, and transformative.
Keywords: power, depth, regeneration, obsession.
House association: 8th House.

## Sagittarius (November 22 - December 21)
Mutable Fire sign ruled by Jupiter. Optimistic, adventurous, philosophical, and blunt.
Keywords: freedom, truth, exploration, excess.
House association: 9th House.

## Capricorn (December 22 - January 19)
Cardinal Earth sign ruled by Saturn. Ambitious, disciplined, responsible, and practical.
Keywords: achievement, structure, authority, pessimism.
House association: 10th House.

## Aquarius (January 20 - February 18)
Fixed Air sign ruled by Uranus and Saturn. Innovative, humanitarian, independent, and unconventional.
Keywords: progress, community, rebellion, detachment.
House association: 11th House.

## Pisces (February 19 - March 20)
Mutable Water sign ruled by Neptune and Jupiter. Compassionate, artistic, intuitive, and dreamy.
Keywords: transcendence, empathy, sacrifice, escapism.
House association: 12th House.
